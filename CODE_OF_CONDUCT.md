# Code of Conduct

OmniScientist contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive disagreement, and
avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of another person's private
research data or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through their repository-hosting
profiles; do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Reports will be handled confidentially to the
extent practical, with conflicts of interest delegated to another maintainer.
