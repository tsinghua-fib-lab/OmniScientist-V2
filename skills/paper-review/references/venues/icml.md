# ICML Profile

Status: official-site-anchored contract for author-facing pre-submission review.

## Official Sources

- ICML 2026 Reviewer Instructions: https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerInstructions
- ICML 2025 Reviewer Instructions: https://icml.cc/Conferences/2025/ReviewerInstructions

## Scope And Version Selection

- This profile covers the ICML main track.
- Use the 2025 contract for ICML 2025 and the 2026 contract for ICML 2026; the forms and scales are not interchangeable.
- When no year is supplied, use the newest verified contract and state the assumed year in `Target Venue`.
- Position papers and other ICML tracks require their own official instructions and must not inherit the main-track form.
- For an unverified year, consult that year's official ICML reviewer instructions. Do not translate scores from another year and do not use the generic fallback form.

## ICML 2025 Review Contract

Render the substantive, author-facing fields in this order:

1. `Summary`
2. `Claims And Evidence`
3. `Relation To Prior Works`
4. `Other Aspects`
5. `Questions For Authors`
6. `Ethical Issues`
7. `Overall Recommendation`

The 2025 main-track form does not publish a numeric confidence field. Code-of-conduct acknowledgement and reviewer-private expertise questions are not rendered in an author-facing pre-submission review.

### ICML 2025 Scoring

- `Overall Recommendation`: 1 Reject, 2 Weak Reject, 3 Weak Accept, 4 Accept, 5 Strong Accept.

Do not add the 2026 four-dimensional scores or a generic confidence score to a 2025 review.

## ICML 2026 Review Contract

Render the substantive, author-facing fields in this order:

1. `Summary`
2. `Strengths And Weaknesses`
3. `Soundness`
4. `Presentation`
5. `Significance`
6. `Originality`
7. `Key Questions For Authors`
8. `Limitations`
9. `Overall Recommendation`
10. `Confidence`
11. `Ethical Concerns`

Compliance acknowledgements and the post-rebuttal final-justification field are not rendered in an initial author-facing pre-submission review.

### ICML 2026 Scoring

- `Soundness`: 1 poor, 2 fair, 3 good, 4 excellent.
- `Presentation`: 1 poor, 2 fair, 3 good, 4 excellent.
- `Significance`: 1 poor, 2 fair, 3 good, 4 excellent.
- `Originality`: 1 poor, 2 fair, 3 good, 4 excellent.
- `Overall Recommendation`: 1 Strong Reject, 2 Reject, 3 Weak Reject, 4 Weak Accept, 5 Accept, 6 Strong Accept.
- `Confidence`: 1 educated guess, 2 willing to defend with substantial uncertainty, 3 fairly confident, 4 confident but not certain, 5 absolutely certain.

Use scores 3 and 4 sparingly. Tie every dimension and recommendation to the written assessment.

## Criteria

- substantiation of claimed contributions;
- soundness of theory, methods, and evaluation;
- presentation and reproducibility;
- significance;
- originality;
- relation to current research;
- limitations and potential negative societal impact;
- ethics.

## Venue-Specific Checks

- Do experiments test the paper's stated hypotheses, and do empirical claims follow from the results?
- Are theoretical claims correct, complete, and based on reasonable assumptions?
- Would adding omitted prior work change the paper's novelty, significance, or conclusions?
- Are major decision-relevant weaknesses separated from minor, easily fixed issues?
- Are key questions limited to issues whose answers could change the evaluation or resolve a critical ambiguity?
- Are limitations and potential negative impacts discussed constructively?

## Calibration Rules

- Evaluate the paper on the target year's published contract, not on another year's form.
- Do not treat every missing citation as severe; explain whether it changes the conclusions.
- Do not inflate significance for technically sound but narrow work, and do not dismiss narrow work without assessing its actual value.
- Reward candid limitations rather than treating them as automatic weaknesses.
- Use an empathetic, evidence-grounded tone and focus on central contributions before minor corrections.
