# Top-conference poster quality

A good conference poster is an expert conversation surface: the main contribution is clear at a distance, decisive evidence is easy to inspect, and the method plus any source-grounded boundaries remain scientifically honest.

Aim for the conference character visible across the bundled real posters and PosterSum-like ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR examples: a compact masthead, immediately distinguishable sections, readable evidence, and purposeful information density. This is a visual-review target, never a fixed rail count, template, or deterministic score.

## Content hierarchy

1. Title and authorship establish identity.
2. The central contribution is easy to find without promotional language.
3. Method explains the novel mechanism, architecture, or study design.
4. Primary evidence receives the most visual area and retains conditions, units, baselines, and uncertainty.
5. Any source-grounded limitation or boundary condition bounds the claim; bibliographic references and acknowledgments remain legible and compact. Internal evidence locators stay in metadata; the workflow delivers production records separately.

Use the existing priority ladder as an editorial hierarchy: `focal` is the first distance-level entry, `primary` carries the remaining indispensable argument spine, `supporting` is for closer inspection, and `footer` closes with scope, bibliographic references or acknowledgments. These roles guide typography and emphasis; they are not area thresholds or a reason to shrink a carrier below readability.

Design a quick-scan layer that connects the central claim, essential mechanism and decisive visual evidence. Headings provide entry points; nearby explanations and visual carriers make the argument understandable. Judge the effort needed to recover that argument from paragraphs, equations and tables even when every mark is legible. Reserve closer reading for necessary qualifications. Select coherent portions of source tables while retaining their conditions, units and baselines. Supporting derivations, secondary experiments and exhaustive parameter sweeps belong in the paper. A selected figure receives a short interpretation that explains what to notice. When a secondary carrier is omitted, retain its point only if the central argument needs it.

## Layout

- Choose an evidence-driven emphasis. Figure-led, method-led, result-led, balanced, and multi-entry compositions are examples, not a closed set of modes.
- Use a compact conference masthead. A three-column scaffold is familiar for many landscape ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR posters, but the reference, content, and figure geometry may call for another topology. Unequal measures, cross-column figures, sidecars, local subgrids, and deliberate asymmetry are valid.
- Respect the requested relationship to a custom reference. Faithful reproduction preserves its observed spatial and graphic relationships; adaptation chooses grouping, order and spans for the new content. Built-in references guide reading hierarchy, proximity and evidence scale; the default visual treatment governs their adaptation.
- Give display-scale hero text enough inline measure. If a central claim wraps to four or more lines, widen its text block or rebalance the hero before reducing type.
- Align edges and use consistent gutters, but avoid a dashboard of identical cards.
- Choose boundaries by local information relationships. Open regions work when proximity, alignment and headings make membership clear. Light dividers can distinguish adjacent reader questions along a continuous reading path. Restrained complete outlines can gather a related comparison, experiment or process when neighboring material makes membership ambiguous. Keep the heading, evidence and interpretation inside the same visual group; use the lightest boundary that clarifies it. Open and enclosed groups can coexist. Assess unrelated groups that merge, related groups that become fragmented and boundary weight that competes with evidence. White surfaces can support either treatment. Decorative single-side borders, accent rails and heading stripes fall outside the default; explicit user direction or a custom reference can supply a different framing treatment within its requested scope.
- Allocate page space through information relationships: keep each heading, explanation, figure or table and reading key perceptually connected, and give distinct reader questions clear separation. Compare gaps within related groups with those between groups. Large internal empty bands can detach an interpretation from its evidence even when both share a panel and the content bounds reach its edges. Assess that reading impact alongside unoccupied page regions, stretched blocks and crowding. Purposeful breathing room strengthens grouping and emphasis; coverage measurements describe extent.
- Let rendered content determine group depth and keep table or comparison rows at their natural cell height with consistent padding. Wrapped labels expand their own rows. Allocate additional area to readable evidence and clear separation between reader questions on the fixed sheet. Inspect row contents and their surrounding space alongside the table's outer bounds.
- Use evidence-driven emphasis where it helps the paper. A focal element may be a figure, method stage, equation, result, or concise takeaway, and some posters may use several coordinated entry points instead of one dominant panel.
- Treat physical type measurements as advisory viewing-distance evidence. Resolve weak readability by editing content, measure, hierarchy, or page size; do not encode a universal type-size pass/fail threshold.
- Support fast scanning across title, contribution, method, evidence, any supplied limitations, and sources. A single linear reading path is optional; locally understandable sections and figures may support several useful entry points.

## Typography and color

- Use a restrained type system with clear reading roles: display type creates entry points and emphasis, while body and table type support sustained reading and comparison at their intended viewing distance. Assess visual weight, width, spacing and line rhythm together; heavy or compressed forms across extended prose can flatten emphasis and make lines harder to track even at a large measured size.
- Derive the typography relationship from the selected reference and user preference. Serif display with sans body, all-sans, and a restrained hybrid are all valid when locally available; never fetch a remote font.
- Keep title, section, body, caption, and bibliographic attribution roles coherent through their rendered relationships. Font families and treatments remain author choices. Warn when a requested local font is unavailable rather than silently changing the design intent.
- Compose essential equations, table labels and explanatory copy for a visitor standing about one metre from the printed page. Necessary conditions and limitations retain readable scale in supporting and footer regions. Assess each complete MathML expression at its base size together with its essential marks; native subscripts and superscripts retain their mathematical scaling.
- Keep line lengths readable and use compact labels rather than excessive micro-headings.
- When a grounding source preserves exact LaTeX, typeset central equations with native MathML fractions, operators, subscripts, and superscripts. Never use an equation image or expose raw LaTeX syntax. Without a complete structured expression, use supported prose or omit the equation; linearized PDF glyphs do not authorize reconstruction.
- Default to a white (`#FFFFFF`) canvas with transparent or white content regions. Color serves intact scientific figures, meaningful data encoding and restrained heading emphasis. Explicit user direction, institutional identity or a custom reference can override these defaults in its stated scope. An accent-color preference applies to emphasis; a colored canvas or tinted section cards require their own design authority.
- Integrate each intact source image's outer field and edges with its containing surface, caption and neighboring page. Deliberate contrasting panels can create clear grouping through coherent framing, spacing and alignment; accidental seams or pasted-on islands warrant visual revision when they materially disrupt the composition. Preserve source-image bytes, full extent and colors, including intrinsic margins, and resolve integration through the surrounding poster surfaces and layout. Use screenshot evidence together with the measured surface chain; color difference alone establishes a relationship to assess, with the final judgment belonging to visual review.
- Avoid gradients, glow, playful stickers, and editorial-magazine effects unless the user explicitly requests them.

## Design language

- Keep the title, author, affiliation, and venue identity coherent in a compact masthead. Real conference posters vary substantially in every other figure, table, paragraph, and section relationship.
- Select header treatment, section-heading treatment, module boundary and inline emphasis independently, within the current design requirements.
- Let figure-led organization emphasize image relationships, method-led organization emphasize its process, and result-led organization emphasize the decisive comparison. Choose open regions, dividers and enclosures from the local grouping needs of each composition.
- Scan sections are information groups, not mandatory full-width bands or one-to-one columns. Any track count can work when its measures, figures, and reading paths are coherent; indistinguishable full-height boxes with equal chrome and no hierarchy are the failure.
- A figure-rich module should use concise interpretation around readable evidence, not several full-width figures stacked below paper-style paragraphs.
- Preserve aligned edges and reading order across treatments. Diversity must come from the evidence grammar, not random decoration.

## Final gate

Close-reading evidence includes source images, native tables, equations and method diagrams
with their module context. Detail selection uses the existing priority and image budget,
keeps distinct evidence regions and omits crops that offer no additional resolution.
Associated observations travel with review issues into the receipt and revision feedback.

Typography observations include the page distribution and module-specific distributions by reading role, with measured millimetres and type-to-page context. Use those observations and the rendered-inspection advisories to locate the affected explanation, equation or comparison. Judge its reading impact in the complete image. A material readability defect receives an exact module target and the existing `hierarchy` or `figure_readability` route; the receipt and author feedback retain its measurements. A role advisory remains evidence for that judgment.

Browser `internal_space` observations describe each module's text/media bounds, leading and trailing insets, vertical empty bands spanning its width, and table-row height/content/inset ranges. Text uses rendered DOM ranges; images, SVG, canvas and mathematics retain their complete media extent. Image margins remain part of the intact figure. Background panels and table frames describe structure, while these observations measure the spacing of the content they contain. The editor carries the three largest bands per module for concise context. Side-by-side empty regions, decorative marks and the reading impact of every gap remain part of screenshot assessment. These measurements support composition judgment at the declared page size.

Review the purpose of copy throughout the title band, body, captions and footer, including small print. Scholarly attribution identifies the study and its published version, including identity and publication metadata; production records describe the process of creating this poster, including generation, checking and delivery. Determine a passage's role from its subject and referent in context. A visible account of poster production requires `revise` under `information_structure` with `content-replan`, even when its typography and placement work. Describe the affected passage and its purpose in the summary. Use its module id when available, or `poster` for copy outside module ownership; the source-aware draft author resolves that ownership. Preserve authors, publication versions, references, acknowledgments, required reproduction credits, research provenance and experimental conditions, including scientific qualifications mixed into the same paragraph. Visual-review conclusions cover visual communication and internal consistency within the supplied images, rendered copy and measurements.

Review reading effort, typographic roles and information proximity together. Material defects require revision when extended prose obscures the quick-scan argument, display treatment burdens continuous reading, or spacing detaches explanation from evidence. Route appearance to `restyle`, grouping and scale to `reflow`, and editorial selection or compression in exact modules to `content-replan`. During drafting, every diagnosis returns to the same source-aware author, who edits wording and composition together in the current HTML. Render and compare the proposal with the selected candidate.

Hard gates protect identity, selected evidence bindings, asset provenance and hashes, offline safety, basic reviewability, and editable delivery. They block altered frozen evidence, broken bindings, unsafe resources, source evidence that does not render, a non-editable export, rendered text/media outside the page or its clipping viewport, scroll-constrained element-content overflow, overlap between independent poster modules, and visible poster elements outside the physical page. An empty layout container extending the page's scroll area does not by itself establish clipping. They do not independently establish the truth of a planned claim or paraphrase; faithful interpretation of the complete source remains the content planner's responsibility. Generic figure area and extent, other geometry diagnostics, type size, figure ratios, occupancy and density supply descriptive context for visual judgment. Whitespace and hierarchy are assessed through visible reading impact; measured extent alone certifies neither. The image-capable review uses the whole-page overview for scanning hierarchy and full-resolution evidence for closer reading. Close inspection of supporting detail is compatible with a pass when the main argument reads at conference distance. Missing, blurred, clipped, low-contrast, or otherwise indistinguishable essential marks at the delivered scale remain actionable. A pass requires no critical issue or directive. DOM measurements do not substitute for visual judgment, and a visual pass does not establish scientific accuracy.
